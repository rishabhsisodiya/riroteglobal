This policy explains what personal data Rirote Global ("we", "us") collects through riroteglobal.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can. If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used; if you don't, it never loads. We don't use advertising trackers.

## Who we are

Rirote Global is a sole proprietorship based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. We decide how the personal data described here is used, which makes us the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"), and the controller where the EU or UK GDPR applies.

You can reach us about anything in this policy at [info@riroteglobal.com](mailto:info@riroteglobal.com).

## What we collect

### When you contact us

If you use the contact form, we receive your name, your email address, your company (optional), the service you are interested in and your message. If you email us directly, including ERPTick early-access requests, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write.

Please don't send sensitive information, such as financial, health or identity documents, through the form or by email unless we have agreed a secure way to do so.

### When you visit the site

Cloudflare hosts the site. To deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, it processes technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you request and when you request them. Cloudflare also runs an automated bot check that may set security cookies. These cookies are strictly necessary for keeping the site available and are not used for tracking or advertising.

### Analytics cookies

On your first visit we ask whether you accept analytics cookies. If you accept, the site loads Google Analytics, which sets cookies (named `_ga` and `_ga_` followed by an ID, kept for up to 2 years) and sends Google information about your visit: the pages you view, how you arrived, and your browser, device and approximate location. Google uses your IP address to estimate that location; Google Analytics does not store it.

If you decline, Google Analytics does not load and no analytics cookies are set. We remember your choice in your browser's local storage. You can change it at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw your consent, we delete the analytics cookies from your browser.

### The invoice tool

Clients and invoices you enter in our invoice tool are stored only in your own browser's local storage. That data never reaches us, and clearing your browser data deletes it. The tool loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you open it.

## How we use your data

- To reply to your enquiry, and to discuss, quote for and deliver the work you ask about.
- To keep a record of our conversations with you.
- To keep the website secure and running.
- If you accept analytics cookies, to see which pages are useful and how people find the site, so we can improve it.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not send you marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Under the DPDP Act, we use the details you send us because you provided them voluntarily so that we can respond to you. You can withdraw that consent at any time (see [Your rights](#your-rights)). We use analytics cookies only with your consent, which you can withdraw through **Cookie settings**.

## Who we share it with

We rely on a small number of service providers. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms, and only to provide their service.

| Provider | What it does for us | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Web3Forms | Delivers contact form submissions to our inbox | Everything you enter in the form |
| Cloudflare | Hosts the site, protects it from abuse, forwards email sent to our address | Technical visit data; emails you send us |
| Google | Stores our email (Gmail); serves fonts for the invoice tool; runs Google Analytics if you accept analytics cookies | Emails you send us; your IP address; usage data from analytics cookies |

These providers may store or process data outside India. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid order from a court or government authority.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and our replies for 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them. If an enquiry becomes a project, we keep the related records for as long as the work and our legal obligations, such as tax and accounting rules, require.

Cloudflare keeps its technical logs for a short period under its own policies. Google Analytics keeps usage data for no longer than 14 months, then deletes it.

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

- ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
- ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it;
- withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it);
- nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to; and
- raise a grievance with us about how we handle your data.

To use any of these rights, email [info@riroteglobal.com](mailto:info@riroteglobal.com). We may need to confirm your identity first. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Visitors in the EU and UK

If the EU or UK GDPR applies to you, we rely on these legal bases:

- **Steps you ask us to take before a contract**: replying to your enquiry and preparing a quote.
- **Our legitimate interests**: keeping records of our conversations and keeping the website secure.
- **Your consent**: analytics cookies (Google Analytics). You can withdraw it at any time through **Cookie settings**.

You also have the right to access, correct or erase your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. You can complain to your local data protection authority. Where our providers transfer your data outside the EU or UK, they rely on recognised safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Security

The whole site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and only the people who need your enquiry to do their work can see it. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

## Children

This site is meant for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will say so clearly on this page.
