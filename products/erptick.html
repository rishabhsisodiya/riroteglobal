See It Work

Want the detail? Ten short walkthroughs of the real product on demo data. Pick a module — each video has narration and captions.

01 · Tickets & SLAs

Every Breakdown, Tracked to Closure

Log a ticket against the exact machine and ERPTick knows whether it's under AMC, under warranty or chargeable. Response and resolution clocks run on your business calendar and warn the team before a breach — not after.

  • Clear stages from New to Closed, with hold, send-back and reopen
  • Coverage detected from the machine: AMC, warranty or chargeable
  • SLA targets by coverage and priority, with at-risk alerts
  • Duplicate check when a machine already has an open ticket
  • Engineers suggested by duty status, skill, region and workload
02 · Field Visits

The Whole Job, Recorded on Site

Engineers accept the job, mark when they reach the site and log the visit from a phone or laptop: what was done, the spares fitted, photos and the customer's signature.

  • Accept, reached site and resolved, each time-stamped
  • Spares picked from your ERP item list
  • Up to ten photos per visit
  • Customer signature, or a recorded refusal
  • A checklist before the visit is submitted
03 · Quotations

Quote Chargeable Work Before It Starts

When a machine is out of warranty and AMC, the ticket asks for a quotation. Build it from your ERP items, send it, and record the customer's purchase order against it.

  • Lines from your ERP items, with discount and GST worked out live
  • Sending locks the quote and starts its validity
  • Revisions are kept; expired quotes are marked for you
  • Optional PO gate: chargeable work waits for the PO
  • Print-ready quotation with your company details
04 · AMC Contracts

Contracts That Plan Their Own Visits

Set up an annual maintenance contract once — cover period, value, the machines it covers and the planned preventive visits. ERPTick raises the visit tickets ahead of time and reminds you before the contract ends.

  • Named machines, cover period and contract value
  • Planned preventive visits become tickets when due
  • Renewal reminders at 60, 30 and 7 days
  • Covered machines pick up AMC terms and faster SLAs
  • See what ends within 60 days, machine by machine
05 · Customers & Machines

Know the Machine Before You Answer

Customers, sites, contacts, machines by serial number, spares, prices and stock are read from your ERP. Each machine shows its cover, so the desk knows who pays before logging the call.

  • Customers, sites and contacts from your ERP
  • Machines by serial number with warranty and AMC dates
  • Coverage filters: under AMC, in warranty, chargeable
  • Spares with stock by warehouse and both price lists
  • Read-only here: your ERP stays the master
06 · Dispatch & Service Rules

Your Rules, Without a Developer

Decide how tickets are routed, timed and billed from the settings screens: regions by pincode, skill tags, SLA targets, business calendars and service charges.

  • Engineer availability: on duty, on visit, on leave
  • Regions by pincode, each with an area manager
  • Skill tags that match machine models to engineers
  • SLA targets for every cover and priority
  • Auto-assign and three escalation levels, each with a switch
07 · Customer Portal & Updates

Customers Help Themselves

Customers sign in with an emailed link, raise tickets, follow progress and approve quotations with their PO number. After closure they rate the job, and updates go out by email or WhatsApp.

  • Sign-in by emailed link, no password
  • Raise tickets and follow live status
  • Approve or reject quotations online
  • Feedback rating once a ticket is closed
  • Email and WhatsApp updates in your own wording
08 · Reports & KPIs

Numbers You Can Act On

Run service reports on demand, compare every region with its targets in the KPI matrix, and schedule any report to arrive by email as a CSV.

  • Ticket ageing, SLA compliance, engineer performance, quotations and CSAT
  • Filter by date, region and priority, then preview or download
  • KPI matrix by region against your targets
  • Scheduled reports: daily, weekly, monthly or your own timing
09 · ERPNext Connection

Connected to ERPNext, on Your Terms

An administrator adds the ERPNext connection on screen and tests it before saving. Master data flows in; draft invoices and stock entries go back only when you switch them on.

  • Add and test the connection from the screen
  • Keys stored encrypted and never shown again
  • Choose what each connection is used for
  • Sales invoices are always drafts, never submitted for you
  • Every write-back tracked, with reasons and retries
10 · Admin & Security

Control Who Sees and Does What

Invite your team, shape roles with a permission grid, give partners a scoped API key and load customers in bulk — with every change recorded in an audit log.

  • Custom roles with a permission grid and ticket scope
  • Partner API keys with limited scopes
  • Bulk import with a row-by-row preview
  • Audit log that cannot be edited
  • System monitor, search across screens, light and dark themes

How It Works with ERPNext

ERPTick sits next to ERPNext, not on top of it. Your ERP keeps owning customers, items and accounts — ERPTick owns the service work.

01

Connect

An administrator adds your ERPNext site from the settings screen and runs a test. Credentials are encrypted at rest.

  • ERPNext REST with an API key
  • Optional read-only database link for heavy reports
02

Sync

Customers, contacts, sites, machines by serial number, items, prices, warehouses and stock come into ERPTick.

  • Signed webhooks keep it current
  • A nightly catch-up as a safety net
03

Write Back

Finished work goes back to your ERP so accounts and stores stay right — only when you switch it on.

  • Draft sales invoices for chargeable tickets
  • Stock entries for spares used on visits

Safe by default: every write-back is off until you turn it on, invoices are always created as drafts, and each one is protected against duplicates and retried if your ERP is unreachable.
